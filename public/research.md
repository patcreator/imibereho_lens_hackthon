EICV7 stands for the Seventh Integrated Household Living Conditions Survey (known in French as Enquête Intégrale sur les Conditions de Vie des ménages).

In the Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI), incidence (H)—also known as the headcount ratio—is the proportion (or percentage) of the population that lives in multidimensional poverty.


Key Facts About Incidence (H)

• Definition: It shows who is poor by counting the number of people whose weighted deprivation scores meet or exceed the poverty cutoff (such as being deprived in one-third or more of the weighted indicators).
• Formula: Calculated as \(H = \frac{q}{n}\), where q is the number of multidimensionally poor people and n is the total population.
• Expressed As: A percentage (from 0% to 100%) or a decimal value (from 0 to 1).
• Role in MPI: It represents the "breadth" of poverty. The final MPI value is found by multiplying incidence (H) by the intensity of poverty (A), written as:
\(\text{MPI}=H\times A\)
